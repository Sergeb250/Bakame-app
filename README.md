Invisible AI for interviews and meetings. Real-time answers on your screen, hidden from screen share and recordings.

**[Use Bakame at bakame.app](https://bakame.app)**

7 free answers. No account needed. Windows 10 and 11.

![Bakame answering "What should I say?" above a Zoom call](https://www.bakame.app/assets/screens/meeting-what-to-say.jpg)

## What it does

Bakame sits on your screen and answers while you are in the moment. Screen share and recordings leave it out. Only you see it.

- **Sees your screen.** Press `Ctrl` `Shift` `S`. It reads the question and answers.
- **Live screen share.** Talk out loud. Bakame watches your screen and answers with captions.
- **Gets work done.** Stuck in a console or form? It walks you through the next step.
- **Meeting notes.** Record the meeting or your voice straight into a note.
- **Teleprompter.** Read your notes on screen. Only you can see them.
- **Answers your way.** Interview, Coding, Exam, Writing, Research, DSA, or General.
- **Hides into a dot.** Shrink Bakame to a dot. Double-click to bring it back.
